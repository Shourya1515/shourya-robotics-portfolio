02 · About

From CAD to a robot that works.

Shourya VuddemarriM.S. Robotics · Northeastern University · Boston, MA

I’m a robotics engineer pursuing an M.S. in Robotics (Electrical & Computer Engineering) at Northeastern University, after a B.Tech in Mechanical Engineering from Amrita Vishwa Vidyapeetham. I like owning the whole path: mechanical design and fabrication, ROS 2 bring-up, SLAM and state estimation, perception and navigation.

Recent work includes visual-inertial SLAM with ORB-SLAM3, LiDAR SLAM benchmarking on a Unitree Go2 quadruped, SLAM and Nav2 autonomy for a delivery robot, and cross-camera multi-object tracking. I’m currently working on a project that applies 3D Gaussian Splatting to SLAM for a robot that transitions between ground and aerial locomotion. Before grad school I co-authored an ICRA 2024 late-breaking poster and built combat robots that took three national podiums.

A

What I do

Take robots from CAD to autonomy: Fusion 360 → URDF → Gazebo / RViz → ROS 2 bring-up → SLAM, localization, perception and Nav2 navigation.

B

How I work

Measure, then tune. I integrate proven stacks (Nav2, SLAM Toolbox, ORB-SLAM3, RTAB-Map), benchmark them against baselines, and document what actually improved — and what didn’t.

C

What I’m looking for

Full-time roles in robot perception, SLAM and state estimation, autonomous navigation, or robotics systems integration.
