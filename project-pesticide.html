Pesticide Spraying Robot

  • Amrita Vishwa Vidyapeetham
  • Course project · Mar – Jun 2023
  • Teleoperated
  • Agri-Robotics

A remote-controlled four-wheel robot that carries a pesticide tank, pump and adjustable spray boom, so farmers can spray crops while minimizing their direct exposure to chemicals.

Build

  • Four-wheel off-road chassis driven by four DC motors through H-bridge drivers, operated by a remote controller.
  • Onboard pesticide tank and electric pump feeding PVC plumbing to a spray boom with multiple nozzles.
  • Dual-axis spray mechanism for horizontal and vertical coverage, with adjustable boom height.

Testing

Field-tested for drive stability on uneven ground, spray actuation and overall reliability.

Scope & Next Steps

The robot is teleoperated only — the operator drives it while staying a safer distance from the spray. Natural next steps are waypoint following and coverage path planning for autonomous spraying.
