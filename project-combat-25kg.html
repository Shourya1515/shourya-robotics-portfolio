25 KG Combat Robot – Drum Spinner

  • 2nd Place
  • ₹50,000 Prize
  • NIT Trichy National Tech Fest · 2023

The heaviest version of the drum-spinner platform: a machined aluminium chassis carrying a belt-driven, heat-treated steel weapon. It took 2nd place at the national RoboWars event at NIT Trichy.

Gallery

Design

Built on the series’ shared platform: a pocketed, machined aluminium chassis designed in Fusion 360; a drum weapon on a shaft between machined uprights, spun by a brushless motor through a belt reduction; and two-wheel differential drive.

The frame was kept compact and rigid to survive weapon-to-weapon engagements, with pockets machined out for weight reduction.

Competition Result

2nd place and a ₹50,000 prize at the national-level tech fest hosted by the National Institute of Technology, Tiruchirappalli (NIT Trichy) in 2023.

Learnings

This build sharpened my understanding of impact-resistant design, stress concentration around weapon shafts, and how much a fast repair workflow matters between tournament matches.
