C.A.N.D.I. — Autonomous Delivery Robot with SLAM & Nav2

  • Northeastern · Jan – Apr 2025
  • Team of 4 · led SLAM & navigation
  • ROS 2 Jazzy
  • Gazebo Harmonic
  • SLAM Toolbox
  • Nav2

C.A.N.D.I. (Compact Autonomous Navigating Delivery Intelligence) is a simulated four-wheel delivery robot that maps a two-room indoor world and then navigates it on its own. On a four-person team I led the SLAM and navigation stack — SLAM Toolbox configuration, map generation, Nav2 integration and tuning — and ran the evaluation.

Robot & World

A four-wheel differential-drive base (front wheels driven, rear wheels as passive casters) with a 2D LiDAR at 10 Hz and a 640×480 camera at 30 Hz, simulated in Gazebo Harmonic and bridged to ROS 2 Jazzy with ros_gz. The test world is two rooms connected by corridors 0.6–1.2 m wide.

My Role

  • SLAM: configured SLAM Toolbox (online asynchronous mapping, 5 cm resolution, loop closure) and generated the navigation map.
  • Navigation: integrated Nav2 — AMCL localization on the saved map, NavFn global planner, DWB local controller and the behavior-tree navigator.
  • Tuning & testing: tuned costmaps and the controller for narrow corridors, then ran the 40-trial evaluation.

Team: Meet Brahmbhatt (Gazebo world), Dawei Wang (sensors and ROS 2–Gazebo bridge), Michael Nunes (CAD, URDF and inertial model), Shourya Vuddemarri (SLAM and navigation).

Mapping

Two and a half minutes of teleoperation produced an 85 m² occupancy map. 92% of loop closures succeeded, with about 3 cm of pose correction after closure.

Tuning & Results

Baseline vs. tuned configuration over 40 trials (4 scenarios × 10 runs):

MetricBaselineTuned
Goal success85%95%
Time to a 10 m goal45 ± 8 s27 ± 5 s
Final goal error0.08 m0.04 m
Recovery behaviors per run2.40.6
CPU load55%48%

The biggest gain came from shrinking the costmap inflation radius from 0.70 to 0.55 m (robot radius 0.12 m) so plans fit the narrow corridors, together with local-controller tuning.

Scope

Simulation only. SLAM Toolbox and Nav2 were integrated, configured and tuned rather than re-implemented.
