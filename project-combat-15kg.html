15 KG Combat Robot – Drum Spinner

  • 1st Place
  • ₹40,000 Prize
  • Anna University National Tech Fest · 2024

The 15 kg version of the platform — a larger drum weapon with a centrally mounted weapon motor and a bumper-style front with skids — won 1st place at the national RoboWars event at Anna University, Chennai.

Gallery

Design

A “workhorse” build: simple to drive, quick to service, and able to take and deliver repeated hits. It uses a machined aluminium tub with a removable structural top deck, a heat-treated EN-24 drum on a belt drive with its weapon motor mounted centrally, and a bumper-style front with skids. The drivetrain was chosen for control over top speed.

Competition Result

1st place and a ₹40,000 prize at the national-level tech fest conducted by Anna University, Chennai, in 2024.

Learnings

This robot showed how much drivetrain tuning and driver practice matter. A controllable, predictable robot often beats a faster but less reliable one.
