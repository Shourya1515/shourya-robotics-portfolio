The Platform

  • Chassis: a machined aluminium box — Al 6061 for general structure, Al 7075 where strength-to-weight mattered — pocketed to save weight, with a removable top deck for service access.
  • Weapon: a drum and shaft in EN-24 steel, heat-treated to about 46 HRC, spun by a brushless motor through a belt-and-pulley reduction on 6001ZZ bearings held in machined uprights.
  • Drive & power: two-wheel differential drive, a 24 V 4000 mAh LiPo, ESCs, and 2.4 GHz radio control with mixing and failsafe.
  • Fabrication & validation: designed in Fusion 360; VMC machining, laser cutting and drilling; static FEA on the weapon-shaft bearing mount, followed by match testing and post-match failure analysis.

Debugging Under Load

In testing, a robot would lose drive control and reset after the weapon spun up or took a hard hit. Running the drive and weapon separately isolated the cause: weapon current spikes sagged the shared pack voltage enough to brown out the receiver and control electronics. Separating and buffering the power delivery — and re-checking wire gauge and connectors on the high-current path — stopped the resets for the rest of the competitions.
